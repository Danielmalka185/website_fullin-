# FULLIN asset catalog (Drive media library review)

Scope reviewed: marketing/media folders (04_שיווק_ומדיה tree + the older `fullin/` media folders). 176 unique files after de-duplication by title+size (250 listed). 108 were downloaded and looked at on contact sheets; the rest are listed at the bottom with the reason. Sensitive folders (legal, IDs, finance, passwords, personal) were never opened.

Legend: type = real photo / AI render / graphic / video. q = quality 1-5. faces = identifiable real person (consent is unconfirmed, so "yes" or "partial" means do not publish without sign-off; "(AI)" = fictional people). watermark = Gemini sparkle star bottom-right (cropped off in the staged copies) or other artifact. Staged copies live in `assets/curated/<category>/<slug>.webp` (max 1600px, q82, alpha kept for cutouts, `#` below = index on my contact sheets).

Observations: (1) Many "photos" are AI renders; the only real photography is the Genesis/Infected festival set, workshop shots of the 24/48 towers and the iPhone shots of the 6-slot unit. (2) Gemini stars sit on ~12 renders. (3) Several screen posters show conflicting prices (18 vs 20 NIS/hour), verify before reuse. (4) Pink areas in my contact sheets were transparency, not a colour in the files. (5) Files `RELINX`/supplier-UI videos are not Fullin-branded: do not use.
