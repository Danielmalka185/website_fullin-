3. Rental Process and Use of the Power Bank

The Service enables an independent and convenient rental of a Mobile Power Bank to charge devices. Below is a summary description of the borrowing and use process:

  • Registration and Account Creation. To use the Service, you must download the FULLIN App and create a user account. You will be required to provide basic personal details (such as full name, mobile phone number, email address, etc.) and set a valid payment method (such as a credit card). During registration, you may be required to verify your phone number and/or your payment method details. Opening an account constitutes your approval of these Terms.
  • Locating a Station and Availability. Through the App you may locate Rental Stations near you and view real-time availability of Power Banks at each Station. Access to the Stations is self-service, and Stations are deployed at various locations for users’ convenience.
  • Borrowing a Power Bank (Start of Rental). Upon arriving at a Rental Station, open the camera on your phone and/or the App and scan the QR code appearing on the Station (or follow the instructions in the App and/or on the landing/instruction page for selecting the Station/Power Bank). After scanning and confirming the action, the Station system will release a Power Bank ready for use. You may see an indication in the App and/or on the Station confirming successful release and that the rental has started. From the moment the Power Bank is released from the Station, usage will be recorded in the system and the rental time charges will begin, in accordance with the applicable rates. If the Power Bank is not released or any malfunction occurs at this stage, you must contact the Company’s customer service immediately (via the App or by calling the phone number displayed on the Station). Please note: the Station will not release a Power Bank if its charge level is below a defined minimum threshold (for example, below 20%), in order to help ensure you receive a Power Bank with sufficient charge.
  • Use of the Power Bank. During the rental period, the Power Bank is available for your use to charge your mobile device (phone, tablet, etc.). The Power Bank includes charging cables compatible with most common device types. You must use the Power Bank in a reasonable and careful manner: you must not open the Power Bank casing, modify it, or use it for any purpose other than charging devices. You must not expose the Power Bank to water, extreme heat, fire, or other conditions that may damage it. Please supervise the Power Bank throughout the rental period and do not leave it unattended in public places. Remember that the Power Bank is owned by the Company and is provided to you on a temporary borrowing basis only.
  • Returning the Power Bank (End of Rental). Upon completion of use, you must return the Power Bank to any FULLIN Rental Station (not necessarily the same Station from which it was taken; it may be returned at any Company Station). To return the Power Bank, insert it into an empty slot at the Station and push gently until it is correctly connected to the charging point. Ensure that the Power Bank is properly received by the Station—typically an indicator light will turn on or a confirmation will appear in the App that the return has been completed. From the moment the Power Bank is received by the Station, the rental will end and the time charges will stop. It is essential to ensure you receive a return confirmation: if the Power Bank was not inserted properly or if the Station is full/malfunctioning, the rental may not close properly and charges may continue. If you encounter difficulty returning the Power Bank (for example, no empty slot at the Station, a technical malfunction, etc.), you must contact customer service immediately for instructions. The Company will assist in locating a nearby alternative Station or will update the system to end the rental remotely, as needed.

There is no additional charge for returning the Power Bank at a Station other than the one from which it was taken, and it may be returned at any time during the rental period. From the moment of proper return and receipt by the Station, the rental ends automatically and a billing summary will be issued to you in the App. Keep the Power Bank safe while it is in your possession and return it on time. The sections below set out your responsibility in the event of late return, loss, or damage to the Power Bank.
